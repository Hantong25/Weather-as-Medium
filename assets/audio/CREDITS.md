At runtime, the demo raises its quiet RMS level with a soft peak bound in memory, crossfades the loop boundary, adds an offset ambience layer and triggers short, softly enveloped snippets of the same recording. More visitors increase the number of overlapping snippets per second and the additional rain layer. Pitch varies slightly between snippets. This is an authored sound design mapping, not a measured rainfall rate.

The volume slider controls listening level separately. The weather phase still controls fade-in and fade-out. The same recording is also used by the six-second sound preview and remains available offline.
