    Visual styleSTYLE

    Atmospheric scattering from the sun position; particle clouds lit by sunlight, skylight and city glow at night.

    Audience and imageAUDIENCE
    visitors → 4 clouds

    One cloud per visitor: it fades in on a clear patch of the canopy and drifts with the wind; as it drifts off or dissipates, a new cloud with a different shape forms upwind. It fades out after they leave.

    Simulation notesNOTES

    The puddle outline comes from the pebble terrain and a fixed water level: terrain below the surface shows as water. Water-level change and shoreline retreat are no longer simulated.

    The canopy underside shows the same live sky as Projection settings, drawn as added light. Projector model, position and throw distance are undecided and not modelled.
